Who we are

About the laboratory

The Thin Films and Nanomaterials Lab, guided by Dr. Mohan Rao K. at the Department of Physics, Manipal Institute of Technology, is a research-focused space dedicated to understanding how material synthesis, processing, microstructure and interfaces influence functional properties.

Our research interests span oxide thin films, nanostructured materials, dielectric characterization and materials for electronic and energy-related applications. Research themes and laboratory resources listed here can be updated as details are confirmed.

Scientific focus

Research areas

From fabrication to device-level characterization, we study the connections between processing, structure and performance.

01 / SYNTHESIS

Thin Film Deposition

RF magnetron sputtering, physical vapor deposition, post-deposition annealing and processing–structure relationships.

02 / MATERIALS

Functional Oxides

High-k dielectric oxides include zirconia, ceria, gadolinia and tantala. Cadmium oxide is studied for photovoltaic applications. Our work examines phase evolution, defects and interfacial chemistry.

03 / DEVICES

Electronic Characterization

MOS capacitor structures, capacitance–voltage response, leakage current and charge-transport mechanisms.

04 / STRUCTURE

Structural Analysis

X-ray diffraction, Raman spectroscopy, crystallinity, phase identification and microstructural evolution.

05 / SURFACES

Surface & Interface Science

Electron microscopy, AFM, XPS, composition, morphology and surface chemistry.

06 / NANOSCIENCE

Nanomaterials

Structure–property relationships in nanoscale materials and their potential technological applications.
